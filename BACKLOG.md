# Feature Backlog

Candidate functions beyond PLAN.md's committed scope. Tiers reflect current judgment; pilot feedback reorders this list, not us.

## Tier 1 — obvious table stakes, fold into v1/v1.x
- **Calendar sync** — per-user iCal feed (Google/Apple). Most-requested feature in every competitor's reviews. Cheap.
- **Print / PDF week view** — clean print stylesheet; the kitchen-wall schedule is still real.
- **Shift reminders** — auto push/SMS "you're on in 2 hours". Cuts no-shows.
- **Shift notes** — freeform note per shift ("private party at 8, wear black").
- **Blackout dates** — no time-off requests during defined ranges (holidays).
- **"Who's working with me"** — staff see the day's lineup, not just their own shift.

## Tier 2 — hospitality-shaped differentiators (pairs with hospitality module)
- **Certification tracking** — alcohol permits, food-handler cards; expiry alerts + generator rule ("never schedule expired certs"). Generic beyond hospitality (forklift, first aid).
- **Manager logbook / shift handover** — the digital red book; makes the app the venue's system of record.
- **Break planning** — legally required breaks scheduled inside shifts with coverage maintained; a generator problem, which is our strength.
- **Fair Workweek compliance** — advance-notice warnings and change-penalty tracking for NYC/Chicago/SF/Seattle/Oregon; publish-flow warning first, full pack later.
- **Pre-shift lineup sheet** — auto daily briefing: who's on, sections, notes.
- **Staff-side localization** — Spanish first; staff choose their language, managers unaffected.

## Tier 3 — scheduling depth (generator inputs/outputs)
- **Event overlays** — one-off demand spikes layered on the weekly template (game day, buyout).
- **Understaffing alerts** — below-minimum-coverage warnings on the grid.
- **Skill tiers within roles** — "≥1 senior bartender every Friday night."
- **On-call shifts**; **multi-role shifts** (host 5–7, server 7–close).
- **No-show / lateness tracking** — attendance history informing swaps and scheduling.
- **Overtime pre-approval** — generator asks before scheduling into OT.

## Tier 4 — staff delight & input freshness
- **Earnings estimate** — "this week ≈ $612 before tips."
- **Pre-generation availability nudge** — "schedule generates tomorrow, any changes?" Protects generator quality.
- **Tip declaration at clock-out** — pairs with time clock + hospitality module.

## Tier 5 — bigger swings
- **Hiring hook** — chronically unfilled shifts → one-tap job post draft (Indeed etc.). Understaffing is a hiring problem wearing a scheduling costume.
- **Natural-language edits** — "give Maya Friday off, cover with whoever's under hours"; the constraint engine makes this tractable.
- **Anomaly alerts** — "labor % trending 4 points above normal for this sales level."
- **Shared staff pool across locations** — borrow staff between venues; the multi-location killer feature.
